## 1. Your Goal: Hackathon Problem Statements

Your project must build towards one of the following problem statements:

* **Build for Your World** – Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before.
* **Push the Frontier** – Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials, and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, accelerate a scientific experiment, or unlock a better way to understand and control a physical system. Show us what becomes possible when these models meet the real world.

> **Technical Requirement:**
> Your project must incorporate Meta's models. Use the [Muse Spark series](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-spark), [Muse Wonder](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-wonder), [Muse Image](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-image), [Muse Voice Transcribe](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-voice-transcribe), and [SAM 3.1](https://dev.meta.ai/models/sam-3-1) through [Meta Model API](https://dev.meta.ai/products/meta-model-api), run Meta open weights models [Muse Glimmer](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-glimmer) and [Muse Spark Open](https://dev.meta.ai/models/muse-spark-open), and code faster with [Muse Code](https://dev.meta.ai/lp/muse-code) coding agent.

## 4. Hackathon Rules

* **Open Source:** Repositories **must be public**.
* **Team Size:** Maximum of **4 participants** per team. Solo participants are allowed.
* **Demo Requirements:** Your demo **must only highlight the specific features, code, and functionality that you built during the Hackathon**. Failure to clearly identify original contributions will result in immediate disqualification at Judges' sole discretion.
* **New Work Only:** What you present must have been built during the event. Startups may build on existing products or codebases, but newly developed work must be clearly identified.
* **Banned Projects:** Projects will be **disqualified** if they violate legal, ethical, or platform policies, or use code, data, or assets you do not have rights to.

### Sample Anti-Projects (What NOT to Build)
Please avoid building generic or policy-violating projects such as:
* Any project using AI to generate and give medical advice (e.g. AI Mental Health Advisor, skin blemish detector, etc.)
* Basic RAG Applications
* Simple Image Analyzers
* Simple "AI for Education" Chatbots
* AI Job Application Screener
* AI Nutrition Coach
* Personality Analyzers
* Sports Analyzers or Coaches
* Anything political or involving sensitive topics

## 7. Submission Process

Solo participants, teams, and startups must submit via the **[Hackathon Submission Portal](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit)** by **November 4 at 9:00 PM EST**.

* Teams/startups submit **one project** per team.
* Each team appoints one **Representative** to submit.
* Public repository access and public video links are required.
* **Video Requirement:** Must include a **3-minute demo video** highlighting specific features, code, and functionality built during the Hackathon.

---

## 8. Judging Process

Judging occurs across three stages:

1. **Stage One (Pass/Fail):** Baseline viability check (theme fit, usage of Meta Model API or open-weight models).
2. **Stage Two (Rubric Scoring 0–10):**

| Category (Weight) | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Impact (30%)** | Real-world potential, target beneficiaries, likelihood of adoption, fit with problem statements. |
| **Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%)** | Creative usage of Meta AI tooling beyond basic integration; surfacing surprising capabilities. |
| **Technical Depth & Execution (20%)** | Engineering soundness, thoughtful refinement, and technical craft. |
| **Demo (25%)** | Working, live product quality and engaging demo video. |

3. **Stage Three (Final Round):** Top 5 scoring projects per track are evaluated live by final-round judges reviewing video demos.
